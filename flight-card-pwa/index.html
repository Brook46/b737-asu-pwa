Every leg you've flown, with crew, times and PF / PM — and where you fly most, nights away, landing G and top crew. All computed on this device; nothing leaves it.

Paste the "Secret address in iCal format" from your Google Calendar. Flight Card pulls every duty period from it.

Sync removes future legs you've taken out of your duty calendar. Past flown legs always stay — they're your logbook. Nothing is ever pushed back to El Al or to your calendar.

Backfill logbook (sector CSV)

Load the MySectors.csv your crew portal exports and every sector in it joins the logbook and the insights — route, tail, aircraft type, block and airborne time, who you flew with, and whether you were PF or PM for each take-off and landing. Flights the app already has are filled in, never overwritten, so anything you typed yourself stays. Deadhead sectors are left out — you were a passenger. Safe to load the same file again.

Route info (diversions)

Airports within an hour of the route, rated 0–10 for a 737. Countries listed as excluded are shown grey and rated 0; penalties add or take points (e.g. TR -2). ISO country codes.

NOTAMs come worldwide from autorouter.aero (Eurocontrol’s database) with a free account whose API access autorouter support has enabled. Its email and password live only in the Cloudflare worker, as the secrets AUTOROUTER_USER and AUTOROUTER_PASS (an FAA key, FAA_NOTAM_ID / FAA_NOTAM_SECRET, also works). Until then ratings say “NOTAMs not checked”. ILS / CAT categories are in no free data, so the airport card says to check the chart.

Links & backup

Logbook calendar (auto-updating)

Flight Card publishes your logbook to a private URL after every change. Subscribe once in Apple Calendar (Settings → Calendar → Accounts → Add Subscribed Calendar) and it stays fresh by itself. The link below is the key — anyone with it can read the calendar.

Social notes (per-airport)

The Social tab in each airport popup shows your Apple Notes airport folder. Your Mac reads the folder every two hours and publishes any change to this feed; the app picks it up whenever you open it. Edit the notes in Apple Notes — while a feed is set the Social tab is read-only here. Your Personal tab is never touched.
